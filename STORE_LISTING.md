## Full Description

### For All Stores

**Image Zoom** - A powerful yet simple browser extension that gives you complete control over any image on the web.

**🔍 Key Features:**

• **Mouse-Centered Zooming** - Hold Ctrl and scroll your mouse wheel to zoom in and out. The zoom centers exactly where your cursor is pointing, just like professional image viewers.

• **Pan & Explore** - Click and drag zoomed images to explore every detail. Perfect for examining product photos, artwork, diagrams, or any image that deserves a closer look.

• **Quick Reset** - Double-click any zoomed image to instantly return it to its original size and position.

• **Enhanced Image Quality** - Smart rendering adjustments improve the appearance of zoomed images with high-quality scaling algorithms and subtle sharpening.

• **Zero Permissions Required** - This extension doesn't need access to your data, doesn't track you, and works completely offline.

• **Lightweight & Fast** - Minimal resource usage with smooth, hardware-accelerated performance.

**✨ How It Works:**

1. Hover over any image on any website
2. Hold the Ctrl key
3. Scroll your mouse wheel to zoom in or out
4. Release Ctrl and click-drag to pan around
5. Double-click to reset

**🔒 Privacy Focused:**

• No data collection
• No tracking or analytics  
• No external servers
• No special permissions needed
• 100% open source

**🎯 Perfect For:**

• Online shopping - examine product details
• Social media - see photos in full clarity
• Research - study charts, graphs, and diagrams
• Art & photography - appreciate every detail
• Documentation - read fine print and small text

**💻 Works Everywhere:**

Compatible with Firefox, Chrome, Edge, and all Chromium-based browsers. Works on every website without special configuration.
